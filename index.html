Rules that keep the month alive

Bad-day minimum (never skip)

If the day goes wrong, do only these three: 10 DSA questions (easier ones are fine) · 5 applications · a 30-minute walk. Then stop. A small day done beats a big day skipped. Do not double up tomorrow.

No backlog debt

Missed something? Write it in Backlog Notes, move on. Any topic you miss gets re-covered in the December revision loop or the January revision phase.

Sleep, food, movement

Sleep 7–8 hours. Eat on time. One walk or workout daily. Talk to a friend or family member every day — studying alone for 10 hours is harder than it looks.

Rejections are data

Most applications get no reply — that is normal and not a verdict on you. Each Sunday count replies, fix the resume, and keep the volume steady.

How to take every mock

3 hours, one sitting, in the same time slot as your GATE session. No phone, no notes, no pauses. Marking: 1-mark MCQ −1/3, 2-mark MCQ −2/3, NAT no negative. After the mock, spend more time on analysis than on the test itself: classify every miss as concept / calculation / silly / time. Log your score below.

Check links: paper and mock sites change their pages. The official source for papers and the mock interface is the GATE 2027 site (gate2027.iitm.ac.in). If a link has moved, search the paper name there.
